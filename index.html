PERSONAL GUIDANCE

A few mindful steps

✓

Air quality is shown as moderate in this demo. Consider reducing prolonged, intense outdoor activity if you are sensitive to air pollution.

  • Check local official air-quality updates before outdoor exercise.
  • Keep windows closed during nearby smoke or dust events.
  • Follow advice from qualified health and public-safety authorities.

This educational prototype does not provide medical advice or verified emergency alerts.
